Frequently asked questions

How do I restore a purchase?

Open the upgrade screen and tap Restore Purchases. Purchases are tied to your Apple ID, so they carry across devices signed in with the same account.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. Open Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions on your iPhone, choose Chain Day, and tap Cancel Subscription. You keep access until the end of the current billing period.

Do I need an internet connection?

No. You can check in habits offline. Changes sync the next time you are online.

How do I delete my account?

Open Settings → Account → Delete account in the app. This permanently removes your habits, check-ins and profile. You can also email us and we will delete it for you.

Reminders are not arriving

Check that notifications are allowed for Chain Day in iOS Settings, that Focus modes are not silencing the app, and that the reminder time on the habit is set.
